# Directory and launch-site catalog

Research catalog of **1,233 unique directories, launch platforms, marketplaces, and social channels** that accept a website or product submission. Every entry has a submission URL.

- **Source:** [maxlibin/free-directory-submissions](https://github.com/maxlibin/free-directory-submissions) (launch list, accessed 2026-11-10), plus the verified no-login table from the same repository.
- **Entries:** 1,233 unique (23 verified no-login, 890 free, 320 paid).
- **Status:** research list, **not** a vetted submission queue. Re-check every endpoint before use.

> Many entries are generic start-up directories, marketplaces, social profiles, or paid placements. Most are irrelevant to a password-security tool, and some are dead or account-gated. A large list is a research resource, not a reason to submit everywhere.

## How to use this catalog

1. Filter by relevance first: a password generator fits security, privacy, productivity, developer tools, and web utilities. Reject unrelated categories.
2. Prefer free, no-account, no-badge directories with a working form.
3. Check whether the link is dofollow, nofollow, or a redirect. Record it.
4. Submit once. Do not resubmit a URL that is already pending.
5. Close the browser tab after logging the result.
6. Keep `link_building_results.json` as the source of truth for what was attempted.

## Quality gates

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Topical relevance | Score at least 6/10 for the target niche |
| Indexable | Page is public, crawlable, and not `noindex` |
| Link policy | Outbound links allowed in the listing or contribution |
| Cost | Free, or explicitly approved by the operator |
| Duplicate | Domain is not already in the results log |
| Effort | Submission is completable without bypassing a CAPTCHA |
